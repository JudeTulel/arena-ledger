# Arena Ledger

A three.js multiplayer shooter on Gno.land with an on-chain play-to-earn economy.

- Pay 2 tokens to enter a match.
- When you shoot someone down, part of their entry stake moves to you and shows as `+1.2` in the HUD.
- A share of every entry fee funds a community pool with boosted reward windows across the day.
- Three maps (indoor CQC, jungle, desert island), three characters, six weapons, three player levels.

Status: contracts are written and linted locally; onyx testnet package approval is currently stalled chain-wide, so deployment is pending an approver.
